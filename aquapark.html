The water park

One of the biggest hotel aquaparks in Sunny Beach.

Most hotels in the resort send you to a water park down the road. Hotel Kotva built one in its own garden, and it is large enough that a week is not quite long enough to get bored of it.

Entry

Included for every hotel guest

Built into the hotel, not next to it

The slide towers rise straight out of the garden between the two hotel wings. From most balconies you can see the flumes; from every sunbed you can see the landing pool. Children go and come back on their own, which is the entire point.

There is no separate entrance, no wristband desk and no closing time you have to drive home before. On a hot afternoon you can be in the water ninety seconds after you decide to be.

On site · free entry

The slides

Four kinds of slide, 22 ways down.

The 22 slides are spread over several towers, so queues stay short and every age has somewhere to go. Ask the lifeguard on duty which are right for your children — the shortest chutes suit small swimmers, the bowl and the racers are built for confident ones.

Thrill

The bowl

The big one: a tube ride that fires you into a wide bowl, spins you around the rim and drops you through the middle into the pool below.

Racers

Multi-lane racers

Parallel lanes down the same slope — the slides that turn an afternoon into a tournament and settle who is fastest in the family.

Enclosed

Tube slides

Fully enclosed tubes that twist in the dark before spitting you out into daylight and water. The ones children ride again immediately.

Open flume

Open flumes

Wide open chutes with long, banked curves — fast enough to be exciting, gentle enough that nobody needs talking into a second run.

Family

The slide towers

Several towers of different heights so the queue splits naturally, and so the smallest riders never have to climb higher than they want to.

Splashdown

Landing pools

Every slide ends in a supervised landing pool with shallow exits, ringed by sunbeds so a parent can watch the whole ride from a chair.

The smallest guests

A pirate ship, in a pool you can stand up in.

The children's area is a separate shallow pool built around a full wooden pirate ship, with its own small slides, water jets and steps — designed for the ages that are not ready for the big towers.

Shallow the whole way across

The children's pool has no deep end. Small swimmers can stand anywhere in it, and the entry is by gradual steps rather than a ladder.

Slides sized for small riders

The ship has its own short slides and sprays, so children who are too small for the main towers still spend the day going down something.

A playground for the dry hour

A fenced playground with swings and climbing frames sits in the garden a few steps away, for the part of the afternoon when everyone has had enough water.

Aquapark questions

Do I have to pay to use the aquapark at Hotel Kotva? +

No. The aquapark is free for everyone staying at Hotel Kotva, for every day of the stay. There is no entrance ticket, no day pass and no per-person charge.

How many slides are there? +

22 waterslides in total, spread across three connected pools inside the hotel garden. They range from short children's chutes on the pirate ship to enclosed tube slides and a large bowl slide.

Can small children use the aquapark? +

Yes. There is a separate shallow children's pool built around a pirate ship, with small slides and water jets sized for young children. Access to the larger slides depends on height, age and swimming ability, and is decided by the aquapark staff on the day.

Is the aquapark open to people not staying at the hotel? +

The aquapark is a facility for hotel guests. If you are not staying at Hotel Kotva, please call the hotel on +359 554 22422 to ask what is possible.

When is the aquapark open? +

The aquapark operates during the summer season, and daily hours depend on the season and the weather. They are posted at reception each morning — please check there or call +359 554 22422 before travelling.

Is there a swimming pool as well as the slides? +

Yes. Alongside the three aquapark pools there is a half-Olympic swimming pool of 25 m by 12.5 m, kept separate from the slides, for lengths and quieter swimming.
