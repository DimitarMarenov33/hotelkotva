Four pools

One pool for every kind of holiday.

The water is spread across four separate pools, so the family that wants to race down slides and the one that wants to swim a quiet length never have to share the same corner.

Aquapark

The slide pool

The landing pool the big slides empty into — the loudest, happiest water on the property, ringed with sunbeds so you can watch without standing up.

Lagoon

The lagoon pool

A long free-form pool winding between rock formations, palm trees and shaded ledges, with shallow steps and plenty of edge to hold on to.

Swimming

The half-Olympic pool

25 m by 12.5 m of proper lane swimming, away from the slides. Early mornings here are the quietest part of the day.

Children

The pirate-ship pool

A shallow children's pool built around a wooden pirate ship, with its own small slides and water to stand up in at any point.

For the children

Kids in the water. Parents on the sunbed.

Everything a child wants is inside the hotel gates, which means everything a parent wants — a line of sight, a cold drink and a chair that stays warm — is too.

A pirate ship in the pool

The children's pool is built around a full wooden pirate ship with its own slides and water jets. It is shallow throughout, and it is the reason most families come back.

Slides that grow with them

Small chutes for the ones who have just learned to swim, tube and bowl slides for the ones who have decided they are fearless. Nobody ages out of this aquapark.

A playground on dry land

A fenced playground in the garden with swings, climbing frames and soft ground, for the hour after lunch when everyone needs to be out of the water.

Everything already paid for

Before you book

Is the aquapark free for hotel guests? +

Yes. Access to the aquapark and all four pools is included for every guest of Hotel Kotva, for every day of the stay. There is no day pass and no per-person ticket.

How many waterslides does Hotel Kotva have? +

22 waterslides, spread across three connected aquapark pools in the hotel garden, plus a separate half-Olympic swimming pool of 25 m by 12.5 m.

Is the hotel suitable for small children? +

Yes. There is a shallow children's pool built around a pirate ship with its own small slides, a fenced playground in the garden, and a baby cot on request at no charge. Ask at reception which slides suit your child's height and swimming ability.

What does All Inclusive cover? +

Buffet breakfast, lunch and dinner, snacks between meals, local alcoholic and soft drinks at the bars and the pool bar, sunbeds and umbrellas at the pools, the animation programme, Wi-Fi and of course the aquapark. It starts with lunch on the day of arrival, after 10:30, and ends with breakfast on the day of departure.

How far is the beach and Nessebar? +

The beach is 250 metres away, a walk of about four minutes. Old Nessebar, the UNESCO World Heritage town, is 1.5 km away, and the centre of Sunny Beach is about 3 km. Burgas Airport is roughly 30 km.

What time is check-in and check-out? +

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is until 11:00. If you arrive earlier, leave your luggage at reception and start at the pool — direct bookings get priority on an early check-in whenever a room is ready.

Is there parking, and can I bring a pet? +

There is a car park on site at €12 per day. Pets up to 7 kg are welcome at €10 per day — please tell us in the booking form so reception can prepare the right room.

Is this the hotel's official reservation site? +

Yes. This page books rooms directly with Hotel Kotva in Sunny Beach. You can verify on +359 554 22422, the hotel's own reception number.
